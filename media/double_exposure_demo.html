How it works
// Three_inputs

Three ways in.

01

Body

The silhouette becomes a void. Light gathers on its edge and the body emits substance into the fluid behind it.

02

Hands

Wrist tracking opens a vortex. Particles spiral in, and it lingers for two seconds after the hand stops.

03

Sound

The beat blooms the rim outward along the body. Loudness drives how fast the aurora moves behind it.
